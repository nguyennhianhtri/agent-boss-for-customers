Use the internal facilitator handoff.
Before

Set up and test

  1. Create the workshop link.
  2. Add the objective, shared context and real sponsor constraints.
  3. Leave the product portfolio wide unless the sponsor has narrowed it.
  4. Set a separate workshop password. Participant link to the room; owner link stays with you.
  5. Dry-run the participant link in a private window before anyone joins.
During · 60–90 min

Move from outcome to decision

0–10Whose work changes? What result matters?10–25Map steps, systems, information and hand-offs.25–40Set automation, approval and ownership boundaries.40–55One use case per participant or team.55–75Review paths in the owner dashboard.75–90Choose validation candidates and name owners.
After

Close with owners and tests

  • Review the full intake and technical recommendation.
  • Confirm the path or record a revision.
  • Name a business owner for each pilot.
  • Record open data, identity and approval questions.
  • Agree the smallest test and success threshold.
  • Export the workshop portfolio.

ParticipantRecommended path: where, why, MVP and how to start. The pilot plan is optional.
Workshop ownerPrivate architecture, AIDF, authority, risks, alternatives, prerequisites and evidence. Participants cannot open it.
