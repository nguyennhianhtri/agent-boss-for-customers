How it works: A solutioning board is a single canvas your team fills in during an envisioning workshop. Work through each section below to pressure-test an agent idea before you commit to building it.

  • Board section

    Problem & opportunity

    What problem are you solving, for whom, and why now? Define the outcome before you pick a tool.

  • Board section

    Users & journey

    Map who interacts with the agent and where it fits into their existing workflow.

  • Board section

    Data & systems

    Identify the data, content and systems the agent needs to reach — and how it connects to them.

  • Board section

    Agent behaviour

    Decide what the agent should do, what it must never do, and when to hand back to a human.

  • Board section

    Success measures

    Agree how you'll know it's working: the signals, metrics and targets that define done.

  • Board section

    Risks & guardrails

    Name the risks early — accuracy, privacy, security, compliance — and the guardrails that contain them.
