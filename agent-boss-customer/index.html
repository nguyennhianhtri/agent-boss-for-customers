What it takes to stand this up

The initiative works best for frontier organizations — teams that want to lead with AI, not wait. Behind the demos sits a deliberate build: twelve building blocks across three phases. Each is manageable on its own — but together they're a real operating model: sponsorship, curriculum, coaches, governance, and metrics. Here's what to think through before you start.

Executive sponsorship is non-negotiable. Without a sponsor who owns the outcome, a transformation program becomes "just another training." With one, it becomes how your organization works.

Phase 1 of 3 · Blocks 1–4

Set the direction

Why you're doing this, what success looks like, and who goes first.

Block 01

Vision & Mission

Why are you doing this, and why now?

Name the change you're driving — redesigning how work gets done, transforming how people learn at scale, and building a culture that keeps innovating.

Output: a one-line mission
Block 02

Objectives & Goals

What does success look like in 6–12 months?

Set a few measurable outcomes. Ours: make everyone an Agent Boss, and stand up a learning-and-transformation engine that keeps creating agents.

Output: 3–5 goals
Block 03

Target Community

Who joins first — which roles, teams, regions?

Pick the frontier teams to start with. Breadth comes later; momentum starts with the willing and the well-sponsored.

Output: your first cohort
Block 04

Skills & Competencies

What capabilities must people build?

Map where your builders are today versus where you need them — from everyday Copilot use to building and shipping real agents.

Output: a skills baseline

Phase 2 of 3 · Blocks 5–8

Design the program

What people learn, how it's delivered, who runs it, and what it's built on.

Block 05

Curriculum & Content

What will people actually learn, and how?

Assemble learning modules, build-path playbooks, and a use-case intake so real work — not toy examples — drives the learning.

Output: learning paths
Block 06

Delivery & Cadence

Through what channels, and how often?

Set the rhythm: hands-on prototyping workshops, monthly community calls, and regular clinics to keep builders moving.

Output: a recurring cadence
Block 07

Roles & Ownership

Who owns and runs the program?

Stand up a small core — a Chief Agent Officer, Agent Coaches, a Program Manager, and an executive sponsor who owns the outcome.

Output: a named core team
Block 08

Resources & Budget

What tools, platforms, and funding are needed?

Build on Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, and Azure AI Foundry inside a governed platform, with clear tool maps and guardrails.

Output: a funded, governed toolset

Phase 3 of 3 · Blocks 9–12

Govern and scale

How you sequence it, how you measure it, and what must be true to scale.

Block 09

Timeline & Milestones

How do you get from pilot to scale?

Sequence it: finalize the plan, run the coaches' workshop, then play results back to leadership at around four to six weeks.

Output: a phased timeline
Block 10

Success Metrics & KPIs

How will you know it's working?

Track community growth, skills created and used, and agents shipped — the signals that learning is turning into real, used agents.

Output: a KPI set
Block 11

Decisions & Requirements

What must be true before you scale?

Lock the must-haves: security and governance, an adoption dashboard, and the governed platform your builders work inside.

Output: prerequisites agreed
Block 12

Risks & Dependencies

What could stall it, and who owns that?

Name the blockers up front — sponsorship, access, capacity, change fatigue — and assign an owner to each before they bite.

Output: a risk register

You don't do this alone

It's a lot to stand up — so you stay in the driver's seat and we coach alongside. We help you get started, then coach your coaches so the program is yours to run.
